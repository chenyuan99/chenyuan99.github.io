The music stays with the memory

Find the recording you remember

Search Apple Music by song or cast album and compare cover artwork to choose a recording. Save multiple album tracks to a show at once.

Listen while you write

Tap a saved recording to play or pause while writing your diary. Gold animated bars identify the playing song, with a static indicator when Reduce Motion is enabled.

Start with a show or explore

Choose Add Your First Show or Explore on first launch. View and manage Apple Music access in Settings.

Music access is optional. Search and saving do not require an Apple Music subscription; playback requires an eligible subscription.
